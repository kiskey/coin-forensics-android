# Curated evidence sources — Great Britain 1911-B Trade Dollar (Prid-21)

This manifest documents the sources encoded in `ReferencePackRepository.kt` for the original v0.2 pack, retained in v0.3. The central rule is **scope discipline**: exact 1911-B evidence is kept separate from same-type/different-date counterfeit evidence and from general authentication guidance. Community observations are retained as leads, not promoted to professional authentication evidence.

- **Numista catalogue — British Trade Dollar N#8472**  
  https://en.numista.com/8472  
  Purpose: series specification lists **.900 silver, 26.95 g, 39 mm, 2.7 mm**, reeded edge and medal alignment.

- **APMEX — 1911-B Great Britain Silver Trade Dollar BU**  
  https://www.apmex.com/product/290067/1911-b-great-britain-silver-trade-dollar-bu  
  Purpose: exact-year dealer specification lists **.900 silver, 39 mm and 2.5 mm** and places the B mintmark in the center of Britannia's trident.

Because the two published thickness values differ, v0.3 (retaining the v0.2 rule) deliberately does **not** assign a hard thickness threshold. Thickness is shown as corroborating evidence only.

## Professionally documented counterfeit evidence

- **NGC Counterfeit Detection — Great Britain 1909B Trade Dollar**  
  https://www.ngccoin.com/news/article/13925/counterfeit-detection-british-trade-dollar/  
  Scope: same British Trade Dollar type, **different date**.  
  NGC documents a fake at **26.4 g** versus an expected **26.96 g**, with incorrect alloy. It also describes inconsistent color, grainy/overly reflective surfaces, poor ship/waves/Britannia clothing detail, rounded lettering, and Jawi text that is too thick and rounded.  
  App use: type-level surface/relief/lettering cautions only. If a user attaches the source counterfeit image, only source-linked non-date regions are compared. It is never used as a 1911 date template.

## General counterfeit/authentication evidence

- **NGC Counterfeit Detection resource**  
  https://www.ngccoin.com/resources/counterfeit-detection/  
  Purpose: counterfeit-method taxonomy including cast and transfer-die counterfeits, and common warning patterns such as weak details, incorrect weight/composition, unusual luster and repeating transferred defects.

- **NGC authentication article — repeating transferred marks**  
  https://www.ngccoin.com/news/article/1086/coin-authenticators/  
  Purpose: repeating contact marks/depressions across multiple suspect coins can reveal transfer-die counterfeits. The app keeps this as a manual diagnostic unless the relevant source provides a documented counterfeit image set.

## Exact-year community counterfeit leads — lower authority

- **Numista research thread — British Trade dollars and how to avoid fakes**  
  https://en.numista.com/forum/topic52795.html  
  Scope: series-wide plus multiple 1911-B community examples.  
  Observations include an overly rounded rim, milling/reeding that does not extend cleanly to the edge, weak/blobby detail, and several user-posted 1911 pieces with suspect weight/diameter. One community-posted fake is reported at **20.25 g, 39.1 mm, 2.3 mm**, with incomplete edge.  
  App use: manual corroborating lead only. A user may attach an image from the thread where permitted, but the result cannot generate an automated counterfeit verdict.

- **Numista community case — British trade dollar 1911**  
  https://en.numista.com/forum/topic79883.html  
  Scope: exact-year community case.  
  Discussion identifies wrong color, extra/imprinted-looking detail near the shield, transfer-die-like characteristics, and wrong diameter/weight in the pictured example.  
  App use: manual-only visual comparison. The source is kept separate from NGC/PCGS authority.

## How v0.3 uses the images

The app has two separate local image pools:

1. **Genuine controls** — only sources typed as `CERTIFIED_GENUINE` or `GENUINE_AUCTION` can contribute to the genuine regional median and surface baseline.
2. **Counterfeit controls** — sources typed as `COUNTERFEIT_DIAGNOSTIC` or `COMMUNITY_LEAD` are compared separately. A documented-counterfeit similarity can raise a caution only when it is materially closer than the genuine consensus in a source-linked region. Community samples remain manual-only.

Failure to resemble one known counterfeit is **never** treated as evidence of authenticity, because a different counterfeit die or manufacturing method may exist.

## Variety caution

PCGS separately catalogs a **1911/00-B Prid-21 overdate (PCGS #207443)**. A target with date geometry that differs from a normal-date 1911-B control therefore needs an overdate comparison before a mismatch is interpreted as counterfeit evidence.

## Image rights / redistribution

The project does not bundle source images from PCGS, NGC, Stack's Bowers, GreatCollections, Numista, or other websites. Source images may be protected by copyright, licenses, or website terms. The app stores source metadata and lets a user bind a personally obtained/local reference image to that documented source. This preserves provenance without redistributing a third-party image library.
